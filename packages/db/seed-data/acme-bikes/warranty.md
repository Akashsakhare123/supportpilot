# Warranty

## Coverage

- **Frames**: lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner.
- **Forks, wheels and components**: 2 years.
- **E-bike motors and batteries**: 2 years or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## What is not covered

Normal wear (tyres, brake pads, chains, cassettes), crash damage, rust caused by storage outdoors,
and damage from modifications or racing are not covered.

## How to make a claim

1. Find your order number in the confirmation email.
2. Take clear photos of the problem and of the serial number under the bottom bracket.
3. Submit the claim on the warranty page. We reply within 2 business days.

Approved claims are repaired or replaced free of charge, including shipping both ways.

## Crash replacement

If your frame is damaged in a crash within the first 3 years, we sell you a replacement frame at 40% off.
