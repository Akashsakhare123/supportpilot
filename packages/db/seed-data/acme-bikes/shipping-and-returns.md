# Shipping and returns

## Delivery times

- **Standard delivery** (free on orders over $150, otherwise $9): 3 to 5 business days.
- **Express delivery** ($19): 1 to 2 business days for orders placed before 2 pm.
- Bikes ship 90% assembled. Fitting the handlebar and pedals takes about 15 minutes; tools are in the box.
- We currently ship to the United States and Canada only.

## Tracking your order

You get a tracking link by email as soon as the order leaves our warehouse. Orders placed on a
weekend are dispatched on Monday.

## Returns

- You can return any unused bike or accessory within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Bikes that have been ridden outdoors can be returned within 30 days for a refund minus a $50 restocking fee.
- Start a return from the "My orders" page. We email a prepaid return label; the bike must go back in its original box.
- Refunds reach your original payment method 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

## Damaged on arrival

If anything arrives damaged, send a photo to support within 7 days and we ship a replacement part
free of charge, or a full replacement bike if the frame is affected.
