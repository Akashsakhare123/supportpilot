# Bike sizing guide

Choose your frame size by height. If you are between two sizes, pick the smaller size for a more
upright, relaxed ride and the larger one for a stretched-out, faster position.

| Rider height | Frame size |
|---|---|
| 150 to 162 cm (4'11" to 5'4") | XS |
| 162 to 172 cm (5'4" to 5'8") | S |
| 172 to 182 cm (5'8" to 6'0") | M |
| 182 to 192 cm (6'0" to 6'4") | L |
| 192 to 203 cm (6'4" to 6'8") | XL |

## Kids' bikes

Kids' bikes are sized by wheel: 16" wheels for ages 4 to 6, 20" for ages 6 to 9 and 24" for ages 9 to 12.

## Free size exchange

If the size is wrong, we exchange an unridden bike for a different size free of charge within 30 days.
