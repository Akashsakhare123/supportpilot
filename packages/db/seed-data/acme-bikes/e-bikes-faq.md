# E-bike FAQ

**How far can I ride on one charge?**
The Acme Volt 500 battery gives 60 to 100 km (40 to 60 miles) depending on assist level, rider
weight and hills.

**How long does charging take?**
A full charge takes 4.5 hours with the included charger; 0 to 80% takes about 2.5 hours.

**Can I ride in the rain?**
Yes. Motors and batteries are rated IP65 (splash and rain proof). Do not submerge the bike or use a pressure washer.

**Do I need a licence?**
No. Our e-bikes assist up to 32 km/h (20 mph) and count as Class 1 or Class 2 e-bikes in most US states and Canadian provinces.

**How do I store the battery over winter?**
Keep it indoors at 10 to 20 degrees Celsius, charged to around 60%, and top it up every two months.
